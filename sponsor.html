04 — The Day

6 hours. One room.
From idea to working product.

A focused, full-day experience designed to take business owners from “I should use AI” to “I just built something real.”

10:00 AM

Arrival & Coffee

Check-in, coffee, tea, light refreshments. Informal networking while the background slide deck loops with agenda, Wi-Fi, tools, and prompts.

10:15 AM

Opening Session: Framing the Day

Welcome & objectives, overview of AI tools (Codex, cloud tools, APIs, prompt engineering), use cases & examples, mentor intros with LinkedIn QR codes, and what success looks like by 4 PM.

Sponsor Moment
11:00 AM

Problem Clarity & Design Sprint

Structured exercise: define the problem (Who? What? Why now?), reframe into an AI-ready statement, identify constraints, sketch solution directions, and map the solution. Facilitators circulate heavily.

11:45 AM

Build Sprint

3 hours of deep building — tool setup, prototype workflows, lightweight tools, AI prompts & agents, real data testing. Lunch & snacks included. Mentors rotate every 15–20 min per team.

Core Experience · 3 hrs
2:45 PM

Prepare Presentations

Teams prepare a tight 3-minute format: the problem (1 min), what they built (1 min), and a live demo (1 min).

3:15 PM

Presentations

3 minutes per team with short Q&A (2 min max). Every team gets their moment on stage.

Main Stage
4:00 PM

Closing

Group reflection — wins, surprises, what you’ll implement next week. Close with next steps & follow-up support options.
